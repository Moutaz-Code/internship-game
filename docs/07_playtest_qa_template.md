## Test Goal

What question is this session trying to answer?

Example:

> Can a first-time player understand the extraction objective and complete the loop without verbal help?

## Pre-Test Checklist

- Headset tracking is stable.
- Play area is clear.
- Build launches from clean start.
- Audio is working.
- Controllers are charged/tracked.
- Haptic/physical device is calibrated or disabled.
- Stop signal is explained.
- Observer can end the session quickly.

## In-Session Observations

Objective clarity:

- Did the player know what to do?
- When did they understand it?
- What confused them?

Navigation:

- Did they know where to go?
- Did they get lost in a useful or frustrating way?

Threat/tension:

- Did the threat feel scary, unfair, boring, or confusing?
- Which cue worked best?

VR comfort:

- Nausea?
- Eye strain?
- Physical fatigue?
- Disorientation?
- Startle intensity too high?

Interaction:

- Did grabbing, scanning, carrying, or activating feel clear?
- Did objects behave predictably?

Hardware/haptics:

- Did the cue map to the right in-world event?
- Was intensity comfortable?
- Did it improve immersion?

## Post-Test Questions

1. What did you think your main objective was?
2. At what moment did you first feel tension?
3. Did anything feel uncomfortable physically?
4. Did anything feel unfair or unclear?
5. Would you replay this? Why or why not?
6. What was the most memorable moment?
7. What should be changed first?
