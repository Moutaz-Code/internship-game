## Page and section geometry

- US Letter, portrait.
- Margins: 0.75 in left/right and 1.00 in top/bottom.
- Three-section layout with new-page section breaks; first section supports a different first page.
- Lowercase Roman numerals for front matter; Arabic numerals restart at the report body.
- Footer page number centered near the bottom margin. No header content is required.

## Typography and hierarchy

- Times New Roman throughout.
- Cover title: centered, large, regular/bold hierarchy with generous white space.
- Body: approximately 12 pt, justified, conventional academic paragraph spacing.
- Heading 1: uppercase section title with numeric prefix.
- Heading 2: numbered subsection title.
- Heading 3/4: use only when technically necessary; avoid overly deep numbering.
- Figures and tables use compact Times New Roman captions below the object.
