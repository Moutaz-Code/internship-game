  2.1        About the Company


One of the mainstays of Emirates Steel Arkan's Building Materials division, Al Ain Cement
Factory is a significant player in the cement industry. With its origins dating back to the
founding of Emirates Cement Factory in 1978, the business saw a radical transition and adopted
cutting-edge technology with the opening of a new facility in 2014. As a vital component of
Arkan Building Materials, the facility has come to represent the Emirates Steel Arkan's
unwavering dedication to quality as well as technological breakthroughs in the cement sector
[1].


Located in Al-Ain, Abu Dhabi, this advanced cement manufacturing facility has an astounding
10,000 metric tons of output capacity per day—a significant improvement over the previous
facility. The opening of a new cement plant worth AED1.3 billion ($354 million) marked a
crucial turning point in the company's growth direction. This enormous undertaking increased
output capacity from 900,000 tonnes year to an incredible 4 million tonnes annually. With this
development, Arkan is demonstrating its dedication to servicing the growing demand for
premium cement in the region and making the plant the biggest of its kind in the United Arab
Emirates. The General Holding Corporation (Senaat) of the Abu Dhabi government owns 51%
of Arkan, with the remaining share being listed on the Abu Dhabi Securities Market. This
ownership structure is what makes Arkan unique. This strong ownership structure supports the
company's strategic growth plans and stability by reflecting a well-balanced combination of
market involvement and support from the government [1].

 2.2     Organizational Layout




The organizational structure of Arkan is intended to maximize operational effectiveness and
foster efficient decision-making among various functional departments. The primary person in
charge of guiding the business toward its long-term objectives and strategies is the general
manager. A varied set of departmental managers, each responsible for overseeing specific
duties vital to the company's performance, collaborate with the general manager.


The supply chain manager is in charge of managing the entire logistics and purchasing process,
making sure that resources and materials needed for production are delivered on schedule. The
IT manager is responsible for overseeing the organization's IT setup and putting creative ideas
into practice to improve productivity and operational capacity.
In order to guarantee that standards of financial management are maintained, the financial
manager is essential in supervising financial activities, such as planning, budgeting, and
accounting. The job of the quality assurance manager is to protect the business's brand and

upholding quality standards, the production manager oversees all production-related activities
and optimizes the manufacturing procedures.

dependability and effectiveness of manufacturing assets and machinery.
When all factors are considered, this organizational structure shows a unified and cooperative
