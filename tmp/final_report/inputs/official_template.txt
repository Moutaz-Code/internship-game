                                        Summary/Abstract


The goal of the internship program as a core course in the Bachelor of Science in Information
Technology, is to provide real-world experience and create a professional working environment for

2.      COMPANY DESCRIPTION

2.1     About the Company
The fastest growing education technology company in the MENA Region. A revolutionary smart
educational platform which replaces the old legacy teacher centric system to a student centric system.
This not only guides the students but also provides each learning task in categories of games, questions,
videos, audios and quizzes.


The company offers students a smart online platform that runs within the school servers that host the
platform. Moreover, they have web-services offered for the on-site support to manage users in the
schools, raise tickets for different levels of network issues faced, and google admin console to add users
to their respective school district to be able to access the drive.
