     Assignments engaged in:
      • Defined and implemented the v0.3.0.0 Readable Stealth and Contractor Counterplay prototype around
        one clear question: whether players could understand and deliberately manage the Owl's sight, sound,
        hiding, and distraction behaviour without a stealth meter.
      •   Reworked Owl vision into three-sample Concealed, Noticeable, and Exposed evidence with graded
          confirmation rates instead of instant binary detection.
      •   Unified crouching, walking, sprinting, landing, and object impacts through a normalized sound-stimulus
          path with distance falloff and an explicit hearing threshold.
      •   Separated confirmed sight, accepted sound, last-seen position, last-heard position, search probes, and
          patrol targets so the Owl acted on credible information rather than a hidden live player position.
      •   Protected full visual cover followed by quiet relocation as a dependable stealth rule, with current
          confirmed sight taking priority over distracting sounds.
      •   Added nine portable metal distractions with single-impact gating, spatial audio, cooldown protection,
          and complete reset behaviour.
      •   Added three maintenance-test panels that create localized machinery distractions, visible active states,
          cooldowns, and matching procedural audio.
      •   Expanded F1/F3 developer telemetry to expose visual samples, confirmation, movement noise,
          hearing decisions, knowledge source, target position, route-adaptation state, and panel status.
      •   Validated v0.3.0.0 with 22 focused Edit Mode tests, 133 total Edit Mode tests, six Play Mode tests, a

     Knowledge gained:
      •     Learned that readable stealth depends on causal feedback: the player should be able to distinguish
            current sight, accepted sound, and remembered evidence instead of treating every enemy reaction as
            arbitrary.
      •     Learned that current confirmed sight must outrank a remote distraction; otherwise, the player can
            unrealistically redirect an enemy that is already watching them.
      •     Learned to evaluate sound after distance falloff and to separate the hearing threshold from the
            stronger evidence threshold used for long-term route adaptation.
      •     Learned that full cover is only useful when the AI also supports last-seen memory, quiet relocation,
            search behaviour, and eventual disengagement.
      •     Gained experience designing procedural audio and local visual feedback so stealth information
            remains diegetic rather than dependent on a HUD meter.
      •     Learned that a map's topology should be represented explicitly. Inferring route choice from world
            coordinates can confuse destinations with the paths used to reach them.
      •     Learned that landmarks and room silhouettes support mental mapping, but connector identity and
            repeated circulation patterns are equally important for navigation.
      •     Learned to use constrained deterministic generation to ensure every run exercises the intended
            experiment, such as requiring one live objective in each lobe.
      •     Improved my understanding of pursuit design: patrol nodes, search probes, connector evidence,
            NavMesh movement, and graph fallback each solve different problems and should remain
            independently testable.
      •     Learned that automated reachability proves technical connectivity but cannot prove that a player
            understands the layout, feels safe carrying an object through it, or considers the Owl fair.
      •     Learned to preserve known limitations honestly. Desktop automation and launch smoke tests do not
            validate XR comfort, spatial audio in a headset, haptics, physical effects, or uninformed player
            comprehension.
      •     Learned that hard scope boundaries and ordered cut lists protect the prototype question when
            schedule pressure encourages unrelated feature expansion.
      •     Gained experience producing implementation handoffs that record authority, ownership, validation
            evidence, open risks, and exact restart procedures rather than relying on undocumented memory.
